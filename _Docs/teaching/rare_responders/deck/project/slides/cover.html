One well: about 1,000 nanowells

The assay in one picture: a multiwell plate, where every well is itself an array of about a thousand nanowells, tiny chambers that each hold a few cells. The deck zooms in from the plate to one well to one nanowell, where the dendritic cells, the T cells and the fold change are introduced, then zooms back out to the well and the plate. The last slide shows how the exceedance test turns those fold changes into one comparison between treated and control wells.
