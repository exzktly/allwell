The smallest scale: one nanowell, number 417, followed through time. The three pictures are not three nanowells; they are the same nanowell at three moments, along the time arrow. It is imaged over time and its T cells counted. Its fold change is the count at the end divided by the count at the start, so a nanowell that went from 2 T cells to 8 has a fold change of 4. Most nanowells barely change, a fold change near 1; this one is a responder. Every nanowell in every well gets its own fold change in the same way. This is the turning point of the deck: from here it zooms back out, first to the well, then to the plate.
