Nanowell = one DC + a few T cells, imaged and counted on its own

One scale in from the plate: a single well, the molecule-A well ringed on the last slide. Two things go into it. First, dendritic cells loaded with that well’s molecule. At top left, DCs take up different molecules for different wells: the colour of the diamonds on a DC is the molecule it carries, matching the plate map; molecule-B wells get green-loaded DCs, control wells get DCs with nothing on them, and this well gets the purple, molecule-A ones. They are seeded so that each nanowell gets one. T cells are added to the whole well and settle into the nanowells at random, so each nanowell ends up with its loaded DC and a few T cells, sometimes none. A control well is built the same way, except that its DCs carry no molecule. Each nanowell is now its own small co-culture, and that is what gets imaged next.
