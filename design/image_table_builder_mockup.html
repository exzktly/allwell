Table Builder

Give each dimension a role — the table renders itself.

Replaces the build-then-generate grid of dropdowns. Your data varies across Wells (chosen on the plate map, left), Layers, Timepoints, and FOVs. Drag each dimension into a role: stack it down the Rows, spread it across the Columns, or Hold it at one value. A Layer is a channel rendered a particular way — raw, corrected, binary ≥thr, cell mask, or outline — each with its own colour/LUT. Any Rows/Columns dimension can switch from Facet to Overlay to composite its values into one cell. Live — no Build step.
